Instead my best discoveries come from a recognition of what’s already going on. I started my Substack not because I wanted to write, but because I wasalready writing and needed a place to put it. My relationships have historically begun not because we sat down and decided “This makes sense,” but quite literally because we couldn’t stop hanging out. This design process can often feel unnerving. It’s been hard for me, someone who’s addicted to authority and control, to learn how to let go. I prefer to drive, and I’ve had to learn how to let my own context drive me. But the truth is, my environment has better ideas than I do.
