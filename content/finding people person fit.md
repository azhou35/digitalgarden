## 🧘 Personal Working Sessions

### ⚡️ Energy Assessment

-  What tasks consistently give me energy?
    
-  What tasks consistently drain it?
    
-  Can I highlight my past 2 weeks’ calendar in green (energizing) and red (draining)?
    

### 🚪 Rooms I Want to Be In

-  What meetings, topics, or teams do I want more exposure to?
    
-  What spaces spark curiosity, even if I don’t know how I’d contribute (yet)?
    

### 🎭 Teachers, Role Models, Villains

-  Who are my teachers or mentors? What worked about their style?
    
-  Who are my role models? What about them resonates with me?
    
-  Who are my villains? What frustrates me about how they work or lead?
    
-  What might others admire in them that I don’t? Why?
    

### 📊 Career Decisions Matrix

-  List past roles (as columns) and values (as rows).
    
-  Assign scores and weights for each value per role.
    
-  What patterns emerge from what I’ve valued—and how I’ve scored?
    

---

## 🗣 Asking Colleagues

### 📩 Core Sampling

-  Who are 15–20 people I can ask: “What do you think I’m great at?”
    
-  What themes or words come up again and again?
    

### 🍽 Dinner Conversations

-  Who would I like to treat to dinner or coffee for a deeper reflection conversation?
    
-  What 1–2 questions should I send them in advance?
    

### ✍️ Mock Recommendation Letter

-  Who could write me a mock letter of rec for a job or program I aspire to?
    
-  What framing or language do they use about me that surprises or validates me?
    

---

## 🧱 Building Artifacts

### 📄 “Working with Me” Document

-  What are my working styles, preferences, and pet peeves?
    
-  How can others get the best out of me?
    

### 🌟 Brag Document

-  What 5–10 accomplishments am I proud of?
    
-  What invisible work (fuzzy work) should I highlight?
    
-  How can I use this to advocate for myself during reviews?
    

### 🗺 Career Action Plan

-  Where do I want to go (1–3 years)?
    
-  What gaps exist between now and then?
    
-  What steps or support do I need to close them?
    

### 🧾 Aspirational Resume

-  Write a resume for the job I want in 5, 10, or 20 years.
    
-  What does this dream job reveal about my values?
    
-  What skills/experiences do I need to get there?
